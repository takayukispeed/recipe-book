# Loaded Beef Nachos

## Ingredients

### Beef

- 1 lb (450 g) lean ground beef
- 1 Tbsp (15 mL) chili powder
- 1 tsp (5 mL) ground cumin
- ½ tsp (2 mL) smoked paprika
- ½ tsp (2 mL) garlic powder
- Salt and pepper

### Layers

- 1 bag (300–350 g / 10–12 oz) tortilla chips
- 1 can (540 mL / 19 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- ½ red onion, diced
- 3 cups (300 g) shredded cheese (cheddar and Monterey Jack)
- ¼ cup (60 mL) pickled jalapeños, or 2 fresh, sliced

### Toppings

- 1 tomato, diced, or salsa
- 2 green onions, sliced
- ½ cup (125 mL) sour cream
- 1 avocado, or guacamole
- Fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges

## Notes

- Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 8–10 min, until the cheese melts. Build two layers of chips, beef, beans, peppers, onion and cheese so the bottom chips aren't bare.
