# Italian Sandwich

## Ingredients

### Bread

- 4 ciabatta rolls

### Meats

- 3 oz (85 g) Genoa salami
- 3 oz (85 g) capicollo
- 3 oz (85 g) mortadella
- 3 oz (85 g) soppressata
- 2 oz (55 g) pepperoni
- 8 oz (225 g) porchetta or roast pork, thinly sliced

### Cheese

- 4 oz (115 g) fresh mozzarella, sliced
- 4 oz (115 g) provolone, sliced

### Spread and greens

- ¼ cup (60 mL) Calabrian chili spread
- ½ cup (125 mL) fire-roasted red peppers or pickled hot peppers, sliced
- 1 cup (30 g) baby spinach
- 1 cup (20 g) fresh basil leaves
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling (optional)

## Notes

- Or use 1 large ciabatta loaf and cut it into 4.
