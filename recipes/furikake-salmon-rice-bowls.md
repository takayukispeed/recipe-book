# Furikake Salmon Rice Bowls

## Ingredients

- 500 g (1.1 lb) salmon
- ¼ cup (60 mL) sesame seeds
- 4 sheets nori
- ¼ cup (60 mL) furikake
- 2 cups (480 mL) white rice
- Soy sauce, to taste
