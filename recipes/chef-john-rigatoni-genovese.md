# Rigatoni alla Genovese (Chef John)

Neapolitan beef-and-onion ragù. No tomatoes beyond a spoon of paste; the onions melt into the sauce.

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 6 oz pancetta or salt pork, diced
- 2½ lb beef chuck, cut into ~2-inch chunks, seasoned with 2 tsp kosher salt
- ½ cup diced celery
- ½ cup diced carrot
- 1 rounded Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- ⅔ cup dry white wine (optional)
- 4 lb yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 2 lb red onions, thinly sliced
- Water or broth, as needed
- To finish: fresh marjoram, pinch of cayenne, salt and pepper to taste
- To serve: rigatoni (two 1-lb boxes for the full batch), grated Parmigiano-Reggiano

## Method

1. **Render the pancetta:** over medium heat, cook the pancetta until most of the fat has melted out. Lift it out and set aside, leaving the fat in the pot.
2. **Brown the beef:** turn the heat to high and brown the beef in the fat (add the olive oil if needed). Work in batches, or crowd it all in, let the liquid it releases boil off, then let it brown. Allow 10–15 min.
3. **Soffritto:** drop to medium-high. Return the pancetta, add carrot, celery, black pepper and a pinch of salt. Cook ~5 min until softened.
4. **Deglaze:** stir in the bay leaf and tomato paste, add the wine, and scrape up the browned bits.
5. **Sweat the onions:** pile in all the onions, lower to medium, cover tightly and leave untouched for 30 min. Uncover, stir, cover again for another 30 min.
6. **Long simmer:** uncover, set to low and simmer gently for 8–10 h. Stir now and then, skim excess fat, and add water or broth whenever it gets too thick. The beef should shred into the sauce.
7. **Season:** taste and adjust the salt.
8. **Serve:** boil the rigatoni until just short of al dente. Finish cooking it in the sauce for a few minutes. Toss with marjoram and a little cayenne, and serve under a heavy snowfall of Parmigiano.
