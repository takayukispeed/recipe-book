# Ragù alla Bolognese (Marcella Hazan)

Use a heavy pot that holds heat well (enamelled cast iron, or earthenware on a diffuser). Hazan's version has no garlic and no herbs.

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 Tbsp butter, plus 1 Tbsp for tossing the pasta
- ½ cup chopped onion
- ⅔ cup chopped celery
- ⅔ cup chopped carrot
- ¾ lb ground beef chuck (fattier is better)
- Salt
- Black pepper, freshly ground
- 1 cup whole milk
- Whole nutmeg (about ⅛ tsp grated)
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1½ cups canned Italian plum tomatoes, cut up, with their juice
- 1¼–1½ lb pasta (tagliatelle, or a short tube such as rigatoni)
- Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, at the table

## Method

1. **Onion:** put the oil, the 3 Tbsp butter and the onion in the pot over medium heat. Cook until the onion is translucent.
2. **Celery and carrot:** add both and cook about 2 min, stirring to coat.
3. **Meat:** add the beef, a large pinch of salt and some pepper. Crumble it with a fork and cook only until it has lost its raw red colour. Do not brown it.
4. **Milk:** add the milk and simmer gently, stirring now and then, until it has completely bubbled away. Grate in the nutmeg.
5. **Wine:** add the wine and simmer until it too has evaporated.
6. **Tomatoes:** add the tomatoes and stir well. Once they start to bubble, turn the heat to its lowest, so the sauce barely puffs a bubble now and then.
7. **Long simmer:** cook uncovered for **3 hours or more**, stirring occasionally. If it starts to dry out, add ½ cup water at a time. By the end, all of that water must have cooked off and the fat should separate from the sauce. Taste for salt.
8. **Serve:** toss with the drained pasta and the extra 1 Tbsp butter, and pass the Parmigiano.

## Notes

- **Order matters:** the milk goes in *before* the wine and tomatoes. Hazan's reasoning was that it protects the meat from the acidity.
- Different editions vary slightly in the meat (beef alone, or beef and pork), the amount of tomato, and the timing. **Check these quantities against your own copy of the book.** One reprint credited to the book appeared in the [Seattle Times (2006)](https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/20060510/bolognese10/recipe-marcella-hazans-bolognese-sauce).
- Keeps 3 days in the fridge, or freezes well. Reheat until simmering before using.
